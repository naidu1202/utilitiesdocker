Frequently Asked Questions

What is Base64?

Base64 is a binary-to-text encoding scheme that converts data into ASCII text using a limited set of characters.

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Base64 is an encoding method, not encryption. Encoded content can easily be decoded by anyone.

Can I decode Base64 online?

Yes. Simply paste the Base64 string and click Decode.
