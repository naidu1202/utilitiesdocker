What is an EMI Calculator?

An EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) Calculator helps borrowers estimate their monthly loan repayments. Enter the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure to instantly calculate the EMI, total interest payable and total repayment amount.

This calculator is useful for home loans, personal loans, car loans, education loans, business loans and mortgage planning.

How to Calculate EMI

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the loan tenure in years.
  4. Click Calculate EMI.
  5. View your monthly installment and repayment details.
