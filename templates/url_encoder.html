URL encoding replaces unsafe characters with percent-encoded values so data can safely travel through websites, browsers, APIs and web servers.

What is URL Encoding?

URL encoding converts special characters into a format that can be safely transmitted over the internet. For example, spaces become %20 and special symbols are converted into percent-encoded values.
