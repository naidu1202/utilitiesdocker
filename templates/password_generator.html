Frequently Asked Questions

What is a strong password?

A strong password contains a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters with sufficient length.

How long should a password be?

Security experts generally recommend passwords of at least 12 to 16 characters for most online accounts.

Should I use a different password for every account?

Yes. Using unique passwords helps prevent multiple accounts from being compromised if one password is exposed.
